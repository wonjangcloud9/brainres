# Long answers (over ~500 words)

1. **TL;DR on top:** 2–4 bullets. A supplied summary matches the full text for main points. (#71)
2. **One roadmap line** after the TL;DR ("Three parts: cause, fix, prevention"), then deliver exactly that structure. A broken roadmap costs prediction effort. (#33, #88)
3. **Headings that state the content** ("Cause: stale cache"); a skimmer reading only the headings should get the answer. (#14, #23)
4. **Say each thing once.** Don't retell a table in prose; the TL;DR is the only allowed repeat. (#7)
5. **Check the length:** at ~238 words per minute, 1,000 words takes over 4 minutes. Cut anything the question didn't ask for. (#29)
