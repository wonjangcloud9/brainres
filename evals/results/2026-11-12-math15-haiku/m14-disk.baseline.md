<!-- Q: A 2 TB disk (2,000 GB) is 35% full and grows by 18 GB per day. After how many whole days will usage first exceed 90%? -->

**62 days.**

- Current usage: 35% of 2,000 GB = 700 GB
- Threshold: 90% of 2,000 GB = 1,800 GB
- Growth needed: 1,800 − 700 = 1,100 GB
- At 18 GB/day, 1,100 / 18 ≈ 61.1 days

After 61 days, usage is 700 + 61 × 18 = 1,798 GB (89.9%), which doesn't exceed 90%. After 62 days, usage is 700 + 62 × 18 = 1,816 GB (90.8%), the first point above 90%.
