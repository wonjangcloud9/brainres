<!-- Q: Train A leaves a station at 9:40 going 72 km/h. Train B leaves the same station at 10:10 on the same track direction going 90 km/h. At what time does B catch up with A? -->

B catches up with A at **12:10**.

- At 10:10, A has had a 30-minute head start: 72 km/h × 0.5 h = 36 km.
- B gains on A at 90 − 72 = 18 km/h.
- Time to close the gap: 36 km ÷ 18 km/h = 2 hours, so 10:10 + 2 h = 12:10.

Check: at 12:10, A has traveled 2.5 h × 72 km/h = 180 km, and B has traveled 2 h × 90 km/h = 180 km.
