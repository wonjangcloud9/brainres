<!-- Q: A $50 shirt's price goes up 20%, then the new price goes down 20%. What is the final price? -->

The final price is **$48**.

- $50 × 1.20 = $60
- $60 × 0.80 = $48

The shirt ends up $2 cheaper than the original. A 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease doesn't cancel out, because each change applies to a different base.
