<!-- Q: A meeting is at 9:00 AM Tuesday in Seoul (UTC+9). What time and day is that in San Francisco in January (UTC-8)? -->

Monday at 4:00 PM in San Francisco.

Seoul is 9 hours ahead of UTC-8 (San Francisco in January, PST). 9:00 AM Tuesday in Seoul is 00:00 UTC Tuesday, which is 4:00 PM Monday in San Francisco.
