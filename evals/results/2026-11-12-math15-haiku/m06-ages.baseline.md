<!-- Q: A mother is 3 times as old as her son. In 12 years she will be twice as old as him. How old is the son now? -->

The son is **12** years old.

Let the son's age be S and the mother's age be M.

- Now: M = 3S
- In 12 years: M + 12 = 2(S + 12)

Substituting the first equation into the second: 3S + 12 = 2S + 24, so S = 12.

Check: the mother is 36 now. In 12 years she is 48 and the son is 24, and 48 = 2 × 24.
