<!-- Q: A recipe for 4 servings uses 300 g of flour. I'm making 10 servings and flour comes in 500 g bags. How many bags do I need and how much flour is left over? -->

You need **2 bags**, leaving **250 g** left over.

For 10 servings you need 300 g × (10 ÷ 4) = 750 g of flour. Two 500 g bags give 1,000 g, so 1,000 − 750 = 250 g remains.
