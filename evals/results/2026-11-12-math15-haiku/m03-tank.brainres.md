<!-- Q: A 1,200 L tank starts empty. Pipe A fills it at 40 L/min from minute 0. Drain B opens at minute 10 and removes 15 L/min. After how many minutes is the tank full? -->

The tank is full at **minute 42**.

From minute 0 to 10, only Pipe A runs, adding 400 L (40 × 10). After minute 10, the net inflow is 25 L/min (40 − 15). The remaining 800 L takes 800 ÷ 25 = 32 more minutes, so 10 + 32 = 42.
