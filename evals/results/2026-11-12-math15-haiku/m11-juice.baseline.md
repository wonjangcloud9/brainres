<!-- Q: I have 2 liters of 30% juice. How much pure water must I add to make it 20% juice? -->

Pure water needed: **1 liter**.

**Work:**
- Juice in the mixture: 30% × 2 L = 0.6 L
- Let *x* = liters of water added. The final volume is 2 + *x*, and the juice fraction must be 20%:

  0.6 / (2 + *x*) = 0.20
  0.6 = 0.20(2 + *x*)
  0.6 = 0.4 + 0.2*x*
  *x* = 1

**Check:** 0.6 L juice in 3 L total = 20%. ✓
