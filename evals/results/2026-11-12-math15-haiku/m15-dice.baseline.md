<!-- Q: I roll two fair six-sided dice. What is the probability that the sum is at least 10? -->

The sums of at least 10 come from these outcomes:

- Sum 10: (4,6), (5,5), (6,4) → 3 ways
- Sum 11: (5,6), (6,5) → 2 ways
- Sum 12: (6,6) → 1 way

That gives 6 favorable outcomes out of 36 equally likely outcomes.

**P(sum ≥ 10) = 6/36 = 1/6 ≈ 0.1667**
