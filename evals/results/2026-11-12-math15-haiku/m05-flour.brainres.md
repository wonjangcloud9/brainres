<!-- Q: A recipe for 4 servings uses 300 g of flour. I'm making 10 servings and flour comes in 500 g bags. How many bags do I need and how much flour is left over? -->

You need **2 bags**, leaving **250 g** of flour left over.

Scaling the recipe from 4 to 10 servings multiplies the flour by 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5, so 300 g × 2.5 = 750 g. Two 500 g bags give 1,000 g, and 1,000 − 750 = 250 g remains.
