<!-- Q: A mother is 3 times as old as her son. In 12 years she will be twice as old as him. How old is the son now? -->

The son is **12** years old. The mother is 36.

Let the son's age be S. The mother's age is 3S. In 12 years, the equation becomes 3S + 12 = 2(S + 12), so S = 12.

Check: in 12 years the mother is 48 and the son is 24, and 48 = 2 × 24.
