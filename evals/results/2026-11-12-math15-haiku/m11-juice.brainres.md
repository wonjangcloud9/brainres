<!-- Q: I have 2 liters of 30% juice. How much pure water must I add to make it 20% juice? -->

Add **1 liter** of pure water.

The 2 liters of 30% juice contain 0.6 liters of juice. At 20% juice, that 0.6 liters must make up 20% of the total, so the total volume is 0.6 ÷ 0.2 = 3 liters. Since you start with 2 liters, you add 3 − 2 = 1 liter of water.
