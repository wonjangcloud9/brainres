<!-- Q: I invest $5,000 at 4% compounded annually for 3 years, withdraw $1,000, then leave the rest at 4% for 2 more years. What is the final balance? -->

The final balance is **$5,001.66**.

1. After 3 years: $5,000 × 1.04³ = $5,624.32
2. After the $1,000 withdrawal: $5,624.32 − $1,000 = $4,624.32
3. After 2 more years: $4,624.32 × 1.04² = $5,001.66
