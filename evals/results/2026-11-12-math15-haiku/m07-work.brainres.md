<!-- Q: Worker A can finish a job alone in 6 hours, worker B in 9 hours. They work together for 2 hours, then A leaves. How many more hours does B need to finish? -->

**B needs 4 more hours.**

Their combined rate is 1/6 + 1/9 = 5/18 of the job per hour. In 2 hours they finish 2 × 5/18 = 5/9 of the job, leaving 4/9. B works at 1/9 per hour, so B needs (4/9) ÷ (1/9) = 4 hours.
