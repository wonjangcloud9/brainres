<!-- Q: A $50 shirt's price goes up 20%, then the new price goes down 20%. What is the final price? -->

The final price is **$48**, which is $2 less than the original $50.

The 20% increase makes the price $50 × 1.20 = $60. The 20% decrease then applies to $60, not $50: $60 × 0.80 = $48. A 20% rise followed by a 20% drop never returns to the start, because each percentage applies to a different base. The net change is a 4% decrease.
