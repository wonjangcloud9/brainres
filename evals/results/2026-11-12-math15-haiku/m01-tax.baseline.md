<!-- Q: Notebooks cost $3.75 each. I buy 12, get 15% off the total, then pay 8% sales tax on the discounted price. What do I pay? -->

You pay **$41.31**.

1. Subtotal: 12 × $3.75 = $45.00
2. After 15% off: $45.00 × 0.85 = $38.25
3. Sales tax: $38.25 × 0.08 = $3.06
4. Total: $38.25 + $3.06 = $41.31
