<!-- Q: Train A leaves a station at 9:40 going 72 km/h. Train B leaves the same station at 10:10 on the same track direction going 90 km/h. At what time does B catch up with A? -->

B catches A at **12:10**, 180 km from the station.

At 10:10, A has a 36 km head start (72 km/h × 0.5 h). B closes that gap at 18 km/h (90 − 72), so it takes 36 ÷ 18 = 2 hours. Check: B travels 90 × 2 = 180 km, and A travels 72 × 2.5 = 180 km from 9:40.
