<!-- Q: Humans only use 10% of their brains, right? -->

No, that's a myth. Brain imaging shows you use virtually all of your brain, and even during sleep, most regions are active.

Three facts work against the 10% claim:

- **Imaging:** fMRI and PET scans show activity across the whole brain over a day. No region sits permanently idle.
- **Damage:** Harm to almost any area has some effect, such as a loss of movement, memory, speech, or judgment. If 90% were unused, most injuries would go unnoticed.
- **Energy cost:** The brain is about 2% of body weight but uses about 20% of your energy. Evolution would not keep such an expensive organ mostly unused.

The myth probably persists because not all neurons fire at once, and that's a good thing, since it would cause a seizure. At any moment only a fraction are highly active, but that is different from "unused."
