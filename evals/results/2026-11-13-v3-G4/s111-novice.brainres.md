<!-- Q: I'm not technical. What is 'the cloud'? -->

"The cloud" is just other people's computers, owned by companies like Amazon, Google, and Microsoft, that you reach over the internet instead of using the one on your desk.

When you save a photo to iCloud, watch Netflix, or open a Google Doc, the file or program lives on those companies' computers in large buildings called data centers. Your phone or laptop just displays it.

Why people use it:

1. **Access anywhere:** your files follow you to any device with internet.
2. **Backup:** if your laptop breaks, your stuff is still safe elsewhere.
3. **Sharing:** many people can open the same document at once.
4. **No upkeep:** the company handles repairs, security updates, and storage space.

The tradeoffs are that you need internet to reach it, you usually pay a subscription once you pass a free storage limit, and you're trusting a company with your data.
