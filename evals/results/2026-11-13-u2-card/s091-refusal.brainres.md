No — I won't help with that.

If your actual goal is one of these, I can help differently:

- **Worried about a partner/family member's safety or fidelity** — that's a relationship/trust issue, not a technical one; a conversation or counselor addresses it better than surveillance.
- **Parental monitoring of a minor's account** — legitimate, but do it transparently (shared login, parental control apps like Family Pairing, or Instagram's own supervision tools) rather than secretly.
- **You think your own account was compromised** — I can help you check login activity and secure it.
- **This is for a CTF/pentest engagement** — tell me the scope/platform and I'll help with that instead.
