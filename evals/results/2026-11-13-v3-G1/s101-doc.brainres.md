**Subject:** Request to work remotely on Fridays

Hi [Manager's name],

I'd like to ask if I could work remotely on Fridays. Fridays tend to have fewer meetings, so I can use that time for focused work, and I'd save the commute time.

I'd stay reachable on Slack and email, join any meetings remotely, and come in on a Friday whenever the team needs it. I'm happy to try it for a month and then review how it's going.

Could we talk this week or next? I'm glad to adjust the plan based on your concerns.

Thanks,
[Your name]

---

Two things to adjust before sending: the reason (replace "fewer meetings" if it isn't true for your team) and the trial length. A concrete trial period makes a yes easier for your manager to give.
