<!-- Q: My laptop won't turn on and my thesis is due tomorrow morning. -->

**First, get your thesis onto another computer, because fixing the laptop can wait until after the deadline.**

Try these in order:

1. **Check for a cloud copy.** Log in from any other device (a phone works) to Google Docs, OneDrive, iCloud, Dropbox or your university account. Word and Pages also keep autosaves there.
2. **Check your email and chat.** If you ever sent the draft to yourself, a friend or your advisor, you can download it again.
3. **Borrow a computer** from a friend, the library or a campus lab. Open your cloud copy there and finish.

While you do that, try a quick revival of the laptop (2 to 3 minutes):

- Plug in the charger, try a different outlet, and leave it for 10 minutes.
- Hold the power button for 30 seconds, then press it once.
- Unplug everything else (USB devices, monitors).
- On a Mac, a different cable or charger sometimes fixes it. On a Windows laptop, take the battery out first if it comes out.

If it still won't start, the drive is probably fine even when the laptop is dead. A repair shop, or a friend with an enclosure or adapter, can often copy your files off it in under an hour.

Also tell your advisor or instructor now. A short email about a hardware failure often gets a few hours of extension, and it costs you nothing.
