<!-- Q: My laptop won't turn on and my thesis is due tomorrow morning. -->

**Get your thesis file onto another computer first, then worry about the laptop.** A dead laptop usually doesn't mean lost files.

1. **Check the cloud.** Open Google Docs, OneDrive, iCloud or Dropbox on your phone or any other device. Also check your email for drafts you sent yourself. Word and Pages often keep autosaved versions online.
2. **Borrow or use a computer.** Use a friend's laptop, a library or campus lab machine, or a 24-hour computer lab. Sign in to your cloud account there and keep working.
3. **Try a quick power reset (2 minutes).** Unplug the charger and hold the power button for 15 seconds. Try a different outlet and cable, then press power again. Dead chargers and drained batteries cause many "won't turn on" cases.
4. **If it still won't start, save the drive.** The files are probably still on the disk. A repair shop or campus IT can often copy them off the same day. Don't let anyone reset or reinstall it.
