<!-- Q: I'm not technical. What is 'the cloud'? -->

"The cloud" is just other people's computers, owned by big companies, that you use over the internet instead of using your own device.

When you save a photo to iCloud or open a Google Doc, the file is not stored only on your phone or laptop. It sits on a company's computers in a large building called a data center. You reach it through the internet.

Why people use it:
- **Access anywhere:** your files show up on any device where you sign in.
- **Backup:** if your laptop breaks, your files are still safe on the company's computers.
- **Sharing:** you send a link instead of a copy.

The trade-off is that you need an internet connection for most of it, and you trust the company with your data.

Common examples: iCloud, Google Drive, Dropbox, Netflix, Gmail.
