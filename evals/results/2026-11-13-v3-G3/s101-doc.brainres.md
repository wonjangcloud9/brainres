<!-- Q: Draft a short email to my manager asking to work remotely on Fridays. -->

Here's a draft you can send as-is after filling in the brackets:

**Subject:** Request to work remotely on Fridays

Hi [Manager's name],

I'd like to ask if I could work from home on Fridays. Fridays are usually quieter for meetings, so I can focus on deep work, and I'd stay fully available on Slack, email, and calls.

I'm happy to start with a trial period of [4–6 weeks] and review how it goes. If any Friday needs me in the office, I'll adjust.

Could we find a few minutes to talk this over next week?

Thanks,
[Your name]

Two tips: replace the "quieter for meetings" reason with your real one if it differs (a commute, focus time, or a recent output you can point to). A concrete reason makes the request easier to approve.
