<!-- Q: If I save $350 a month, how long until I have $20,000? Ignore interest. -->

$20,000 ÷ $350 ≈ 57.14, so you need **58 months** (4 years 10 months) to reach $20,000.

After 57 months you'll have $19,950, which is $50 short. The 58th deposit takes you to $20,300.

If you want to hit $20,000 in exactly 5 years (60 months), you'd need $20,000 ÷ 60 ≈ $333.34 a month, so your current $350 already clears that.
