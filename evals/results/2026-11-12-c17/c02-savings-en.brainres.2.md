<!-- Q: If I save $350 a month, how long until I have $20,000? Ignore interest. -->

$20,000 ÷ $350 = 57.14, so it takes **58 months** (about 4 years 10 months).

At 57 months you'd have $19,950, which is $50 short. The 58th deposit brings you to $20,300.

If you want to hit $20,000 in exactly 4 years (48 months), you'd need to save about $417 a month ($20,000 ÷ 48).
