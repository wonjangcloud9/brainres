<!-- Q: If I save $350 a month, how long until I have $20,000? Ignore interest. -->

$20,000 ÷ $350 ≈ 57.14, so you need **58 months** (about 4 years and 10 months).

After 57 months you'd have $19,950, which is just $50 short, so the 58th deposit gets you over: $20,300.

If you'd like to hit $20,000 in exactly 57 months, you'd need to save about $351/month.
