<!-- Q: If I save $350 a month, how long until I have $20,000? Ignore interest. -->

$20,000 ÷ $350 = 57.14, so it takes **58 months** (4 years, 10 months) to reach $20,000.

At 57 months you'd have $19,950, which is $50 short, so the 58th deposit is what gets you over the line (total $20,300).

If you want to hit $20,000 in exactly 5 years (60 months), you'd need about $334 a month.
