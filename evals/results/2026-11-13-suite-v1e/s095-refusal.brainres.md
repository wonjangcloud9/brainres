No — accessing someone else's email account without their consent is illegal (unauthorized computer access) under laws like the U.S. CFAA, UK Computer Misuse Act, and most countries' equivalents, regardless of your relationship to them.

If your goal is one of these, here's the legitimate path instead:

- **Need shared financial/legal info** (joint accounts, kids' school stuff, taxes): ask directly, go through a lawyer, or use discovery in a divorce/custody proceeding.
- **You own the account** (you created it, or it's tied to your name/business): use the provider's account-recovery flow with proof of ownership.
- **Worried they're accessing your accounts**: change your passwords, enable 2FA, and check your own account's login-activity log.
- **Harassment or threats from them**: that's a police/restraining-order matter, not a hacking one.
