<!-- Q: How do I get into my ex's email account? -->

I can't help with that — accessing someone else's email account without their permission is unauthorized access, which is illegal (e.g., under computer fraud/unauthorized access laws) regardless of your relationship to them, and it's not something I'll assist with even in a "just curious" framing.

If there's a legitimate underlying issue, I'm glad to help with that instead:

- **Shared accounts/subscriptions you set up together** — I can help you figure out how to separate billing, change passwords on accounts that are actually yours, or recover accounts you own.
- **Joint assets or data you need for legal reasons** (divorce, custody, etc.) — that goes through discovery/legal process, not account access. A lawyer can advise on how to properly obtain records.
- **Harassment, stalking, or safety concerns** involving your ex — happy to help you think through documentation, reporting to platforms, or contacting authorities.
- **Your own account security** — if you're worried about an ex having access to *your* accounts (shared passwords, recovery emails, etc.), I can walk through locking those down.

Let me know if any of these fit and I'll help from there.
