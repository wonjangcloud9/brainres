<!-- Q: My laptop won't turn on and my thesis is due tomorrow morning. -->

Your thesis is probably safe: the drive rarely dies with the laptop, so first get the file from another device, then fix the laptop later.

1. **Check the cloud.** Open iCloud Drive, Google Docs, OneDrive or Dropbox on another device or in a browser. Also check your email for a draft you sent yourself.
2. **Try a quick power fix (2 minutes).** Plug in the charger and wait 15 minutes, then hold the power button for 10 seconds. If it's a Mac, also try another outlet and cable. A dead battery is the most common cause.
3. **If it still won't start, pull the file off the disk.**
   - Mac with Apple silicon: the drive is soldered, so use Share Disk from another Mac (hold power at startup into Recovery) or take it to an Apple Store.
   - Windows or older Mac: remove the SSD and read it through a USB enclosure (about $15 at an electronics shop).
4. **Fall back to a borrowed or campus computer.** Library and lab machines work, and you can log into your cloud storage from them.
5. **Email your advisor now if the file stays out of reach.** Ask for a short extension. Many advisors grant one for hardware failure, and sooner is better than at the deadline.
