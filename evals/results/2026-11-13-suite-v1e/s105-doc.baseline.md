# Release Notes: v2.1

**Released:** November 13, 2026

## What's New

### 🌙 Dark Mode
v2.1 introduces a dark theme. Switch to it in Settings, or let it follow your system appearance automatically. It's easier on the eyes in low-light environments and can help reduce screen glare during long sessions.

### ⚡ Faster Search
Search has been optimized to return results noticeably quicker, especially with large datasets. You'll spend less time waiting and more time finding what you need.

## Bug Fixes

### 🔐 Login Issue Resolved
Fixed a bug that could prevent some users from logging in successfully. If you were affected, you should now be able to sign in normally. No action is needed on your part.

## Upgrading
Update to v2.1 through your usual channel. Your existing settings and data are preserved.
