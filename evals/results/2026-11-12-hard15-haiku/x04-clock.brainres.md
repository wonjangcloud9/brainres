<!-- Q: A clock shows 3:40. What is the smaller angle between the hour and minute hands, in degrees? -->

**130 degrees** is the smaller angle at 3:40.

The minute hand sits at 240° (40 × 6°). The hour hand sits at 110° (90° for 3 o'clock, plus 20° because it moves 0.5° per minute over 40 minutes). The difference is 240° − 110° = 130°, which is less than 180°, so it is the smaller angle.
