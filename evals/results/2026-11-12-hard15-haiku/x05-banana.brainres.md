<!-- Q: How many distinct arrangements are there of the letters in BANANA? -->

There are **60** distinct arrangements.

BANANA has 6 letters, with A appearing 3 times, N 2 times, and B once. Swapping identical letters doesn't create a new arrangement, so divide the total orderings by the orderings within each repeated group:

6! / (3! × 2! × 1!) = 720 / 12 = 60
