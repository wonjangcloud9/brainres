<!-- Q: Five people each put a hat in a box and each takes one back at random. In how many of the possible outcomes does nobody get their own hat? -->

This is a derangement problem. The number of permutations of 5 items with no fixed points is

D₅ = 5! · (1 − 1/1! + 1/2! − 1/3! + 1/4! − 1/5!) = 120 · (1 − 1 + 1/2 − 1/6 + 1/24 − 1/120) = 120 − 120 + 60 − 20 + 5 − 1 = **44**

Out of the 5! = 120 equally likely outcomes, **44** have nobody receiving their own hat.
