<!-- Q: A rectangle has perimeter 46 and a diagonal of length 17. What is its area? -->

The area is **120**.

The perimeter gives a + b = 23, and the diagonal gives a² + b² = 289. Since (a + b)² = a² + b² + 2ab, we get 529 = 289 + 2ab, so ab = 120.

The sides are 15 and 8: 15 + 8 = 23 and 15² + 8² = 225 + 64 = 289.
