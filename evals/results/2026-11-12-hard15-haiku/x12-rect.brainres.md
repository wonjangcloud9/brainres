<!-- Q: A rectangle has perimeter 46 and a diagonal of length 17. What is its area? -->

The area is **120**.

Half the perimeter is 23, so length + width = 23. The diagonal gives length² + width² = 17² = 289. Squaring the sum: 23² = 289 + 2·(length·width), so 2·(length·width) = 240 and length·width = 120.

The sides are 15 and 8: 15 + 8 = 23 and 15² + 8² = 289.
