<!-- Q: What is the probability of getting at least one six in four rolls of a fair die? Give a percentage to one decimal place. -->

The probability is **51.8%**.

It's easier to find the chance of no sixes and subtract it from 1. Each roll avoids a six with probability 5/6, so over four rolls:

1 − (5/6)⁴ = 1 − 625/1296 = 671/1296 ≈ 0.5177
