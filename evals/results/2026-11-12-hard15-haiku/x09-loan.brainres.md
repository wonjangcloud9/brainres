<!-- Q: A $10,000 loan at 6% annual interest, compounded monthly, is repaid in 24 equal monthly payments. What is the monthly payment, to the cent? -->

The monthly payment is **$443.21**.

Using the standard amortization formula, with monthly rate r = 0.06/12 = 0.005 and n = 24 payments:

Payment = P·r / (1 − (1 + r)⁻ⁿ) = 10,000 × 0.005 / (1 − 1.005⁻²⁴) = 50 / 0.1128143 ≈ 443.2061

Over 24 months you repay about $10,637.06 in total, so about $637 of that is interest.
