<!-- Q: A $10,000 loan at 6% annual interest, compounded monthly, is repaid in 24 equal monthly payments. What is the monthly payment, to the cent? -->

The monthly payment is **$443.21**.

Using the amortization formula with P = $10,000, monthly rate r = 0.06/12 = 0.005, and n = 24:

$$M = \frac{P \cdot r}{1 - (1+r)^{-n}} = \frac{10000 \times 0.005}{1 - 1.005^{-24}}$$

Since $1.005^{24} \approx 1.1271598$, we have $1.005^{-24} \approx 0.8871857$, so the denominator is about $0.1128143$. That gives $M = 50 / 0.1128143 \approx 443.21$.
