<!-- Q: If today is a Wednesday, what day of the week will it be 1000 days from now? -->

**Tuesday**, if today is Wednesday. But today's date is 2026-11-12, which is a **Thursday**, so 1,000 days from today is a **Wednesday**, August 8, 2029.

1,000 leaves a remainder of 6 when divided by 7, so the weekday shifts forward by 6 days. Thursday plus 6 is Wednesday.
