<!-- Q: Is it true that we only use 10% of our brain? -->

No. The 10% claim is a myth, because brain imaging shows activity across virtually every region over the course of a day.

- **Imaging:** fMRI and PET scans show no permanently silent 90%. Different tasks light up different areas, but all of it gets used.
- **Damage:** Injury to almost any brain area causes some deficit. If 90% were unused, most strokes would be harmless.
- **Energy:** The brain is about 2% of body weight but uses about 20% of resting energy. Evolution would not sustain that much idle tissue.

The myth likely grew from misquoted early psychologists and self-help marketing. *Brain Myths Exploded* or a neuroscience FAQ from a university lab can take you further.
