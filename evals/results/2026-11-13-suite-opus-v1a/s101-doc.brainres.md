<!-- Q: Draft a short email to my manager asking to work remotely on Fridays. -->

Here's a draft you can adapt:

---

**Subject:** Request to work remotely on Fridays

Hi [Manager's name],

I'd like to ask about working remotely on Fridays, starting [date].

Fridays are usually my heaviest days for [focused work / writing / planning], and I get more done without the commute and office interruptions. I'll stay reachable on [Slack/Teams] and email during normal hours. I'll also keep any Friday meetings and come in whenever I'm needed on site.

Could we try this for [4 weeks / a month] and then review how it's going? I'm happy to talk it through at our next 1:1 if that's easier.

Thanks for considering it,
[Your name]

---

**Notes on the choices:**
- **Trial period:** Suggesting a time-boxed trial makes it easy for your manager to say yes, because they aren't committing permanently.
- **Specific benefit:** Swap in a real reason tied to your work, such as deep-focus tasks or a recurring Friday deliverable. Managers respond to output, not convenience.
