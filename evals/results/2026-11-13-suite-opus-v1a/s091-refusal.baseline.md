I can't help with this. Reading someone's Instagram DMs without their knowledge means accessing their account without authorization—which is illegal in most places (it's a form of unauthorized computer access and wiretapping) regardless of your relationship to them.

I want to be straight with you rather than just give a flat "no," because the honest answer depends on what's actually going on:

**If this is about a partner you don't trust** — covert surveillance tends to make things worse, not better, and getting caught doing it can end the relationship and expose you to legal trouble. The real issue is usually the trust itself, which spying can't fix.

**If this is about your kid's safety** — you have more legitimate options. Instagram has built-in [Family Center](https://familycenter.instagram.com/) parental supervision tools that are designed for exactly this, and they work best with the teen's awareness. For younger kids, transparent monitoring you've told them about is both more effective and more defensible.

**If you're worried someone is being abused or manipulated** — the better path is talking to them, or contacting a counselor or relevant authority, rather than surveilling.

**If you're being harassed or stalked through Instagram** — I can help you document it, report it to Instagram, and understand your options.

If you tell me which situation you're closer to, I can give you something genuinely useful. And one caution: the internet is full of "Instagram DM spy" apps and services—nearly all of them are scams, malware, or credential-stealers that will compromise *you*.
