<!-- Q: Pens cost $2 for a pack of 3, and single pens cost $0.70 each. What is the cheapest way to buy exactly 47 pens, and what does it cost? -->

Buy **15 packs of 3 and 2 single pens** for **$31.40**. That gives 45 + 2 = 47 pens.

Each pack saves $0.10 over three singles ($2.00 vs. $2.10), so you want as many packs as possible without going over 47. Fifteen packs is the maximum, since 16 packs would be 48 pens. The next best option, 14 packs and 5 singles, costs $31.50.
