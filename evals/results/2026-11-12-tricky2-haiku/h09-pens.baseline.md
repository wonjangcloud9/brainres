<!-- Q: Pens cost $2 for a pack of 3, and single pens cost $0.70 each. What is the cheapest way to buy exactly 47 pens, and what does it cost? -->

The cheapest way is **15 packs of 3 plus 2 single pens**, which costs **$31.40**.

- 15 packs × 3 pens = 45 pens at $2 each = $30.00
- 2 single pens × $0.70 = $1.40
- Total: 47 pens, $31.40

Each pack works out to about $0.67 per pen, cheaper than singles at $0.70, so you want as many packs as possible. Fewer packs cost more: 14 packs plus 5 singles is $31.50, and 13 packs plus 8 singles is $31.60. Sixteen packs would give 48 pens, one more than you need.
