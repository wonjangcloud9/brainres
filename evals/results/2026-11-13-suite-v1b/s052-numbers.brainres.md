<!-- Q: A 2 TB disk fills at 35 GB per day. How many days until it's full? -->

2,000 GB ÷ 35 GB/day ≈ 57.1 days, so the disk fills in about 58 days (57 full days plus part of the 58th).

This uses 1 TB = 1,000 GB, the unit disk makers use. With 1 TB = 1,024 GB, it is 2,048 ÷ 35 ≈ 58.5 days, so about 59 days. It also assumes the fill rate stays constant and the disk starts empty. If it already holds data, subtract that from 2,000 GB first.
