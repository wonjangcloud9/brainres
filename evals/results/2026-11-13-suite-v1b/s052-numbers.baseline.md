<!-- Q: A 2 TB disk fills at 35 GB per day. How many days until it's full? -->

About **58 days** to fill from empty.

- Using decimal units (1 TB = 1,000 GB): 2,000 / 35 ≈ 57.1 days, so it fills during day 58.
- Using binary units (1 TB = 1,024 GB): 2,048 / 35 ≈ 58.5 days, so it fills during day 59.

If the disk already has data on it, subtract the used space from the total first: days = (2,000 − used GB) / 35.
