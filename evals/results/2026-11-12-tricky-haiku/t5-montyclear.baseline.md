<!-- Q: Monty Hall, but the doors are transparent: you can see the car behind door 1. You pick door 1. The host opens door 3 to show a goat and offers a switch to door 2. Should you switch? -->

**No, don't switch. Stay with door 1.**

You can see the car behind door 1, so it's the car. Door 2 must have a goat, since there's only one car. Switching to door 2 means you get a goat.

The host's offer changes nothing. Their choice of door 3 was always going to be a goat, because the only other door without the car is door 2. Opening door 3 reveals no new information about door 1.
