<!-- Q: If 5 machines take 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, how long would 100 machines take to make 100 widgets? -->

5 minutes. Each machine makes one widget in 5 minutes, and all 100 machines work in parallel, so 100 widgets take 100 machines the same 5 minutes.

This assumes each machine makes one widget at a time and the machines don't compete for shared resources. The puzzle's answer is 5 minutes, not 100 minutes.
