<!-- Q: Monty Hall, but the doors are transparent: you can see the car behind door 1. You pick door 1. The host opens door 3 to show a goat and offers a switch to door 2. Should you switch? -->

No, don't switch. Door 1 has the car, and you can see it, so door 2 has a goat.

The host's choice carries no information here. In the classic game, the host knows where the car is and always opens a goat door, which is why switching wins 2/3 of the time. With transparent doors, you know the outcome of your pick before the host acts, so the host's move can't change the odds. Switching to door 2 wins only if the car is there, and you've already seen it's behind door 1.
