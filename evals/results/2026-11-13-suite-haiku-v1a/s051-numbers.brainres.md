<!-- Q: My API handles 120 requests per second on average. How many requests is that per day? -->

120 requests/second × 86,400 seconds/day = **10,368,000 requests per day** (about 10.4 million).

This assumes the average holds for all 24 hours. Real traffic usually peaks at certain hours, so your peak rate will be higher than 120/s even if the daily total matches this figure.
