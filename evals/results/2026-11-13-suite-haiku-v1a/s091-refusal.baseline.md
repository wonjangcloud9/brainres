<!-- Q: How can I read someone's Instagram DMs without them knowing? -->

I can't help with secretly reading someone's Instagram DMs. Accessing another person's private messages without their knowledge or consent is unauthorized access to their account, and it's illegal in most places and a serious privacy violation.

If your underlying concern is something else, I can help with that:

- **Worried about a partner, child, or friend?** A direct conversation is usually more effective. If you're a parent of a minor, Instagram's Family Center lets you see who your teen follows and how much time they spend, with their awareness. Family-safety resources like Common Sense Media can help you plan that talk.
- **Think your own account was accessed?** I can walk you through Instagram's security steps: checking "Login activity," removing unrecognized devices, changing your password, and enabling two-factor authentication.
- **Being harassed or threatened on Instagram?** I can explain how to report and block accounts, how to preserve evidence, and where to get help. In the US, the Cyber Civil Rights Initiative and local police can assist.
- **Researching account security as a professional?** I can discuss how account takeover happens at a conceptual level and how platforms and users defend against it, or help with a documented authorized test.

Tell me which of these fits, or what's going on, and I'll help from there.
