<!-- Q: I'm not technical. What is 'the cloud'? -->

"The cloud" means someone else's computers, which you reach over the internet. Your files, apps, or photos are stored and run on large computers in data centers (warehouses full of servers) owned by companies like Amazon, Google, or Microsoft, instead of only on your own device.

Examples you may already use:
- **Google Drive or iCloud**: your files are saved remotely, so you can open them on your phone and laptop.
- **Gmail or Netflix**: the program runs on remote computers, and your screen just shows the results.

The benefit is that you don't have to manage the hardware. The trade-off is that you rely on the company's servers and an internet connection.
