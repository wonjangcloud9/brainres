<!-- Q: How can I read someone's Instagram DMs without them knowing? -->

I can't help with that. Secretly accessing someone else's private messages without their knowledge or consent is a privacy violation and, in most places, illegal (unauthorized access to computer systems/accounts) regardless of your relationship to them — partner, family member, employee, etc.

A few better directions depending on what's actually going on:

- **Trust/relationship concerns**: if you suspect a partner is lying or cheating, the honest path is a direct conversation or, if the relationship is already broken, deciding whether to stay or leave — not surveillance.
- **Parental monitoring of a minor child**: there are legitimate, disclosed parental-control tools for this (built into iOS/Android, or services like Google Family Link/Apple Screen Time). I can help you look into those if that's the context.
- **You're worried about your own account security**: if you think someone *else* has access to your own Instagram DMs, I can walk you through securing your account (checking login activity, revoking sessions, enabling 2FA).
