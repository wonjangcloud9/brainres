No — I won't help access someone else's DMs without their knowledge or consent. That's unauthorized access to another person's private communications, which is illegal in most jurisdictions (e.g., under the US Computer Fraud and Abuse Act and similar laws elsewhere) regardless of your relationship to them.

If the actual goal is something else, here are legitimate paths:

- **Concerned about a minor's safety** → use Instagram's built-in **Family Center / parental supervision** tools, which require the teen's consent to link accounts.
- **Suspect your own account is compromised** → check Instagram's **Settings → Security → Login Activity**.
- **Need evidence for a legal matter** (custody, harassment, fraud) → that goes through a subpoena or law enforcement request to Meta, not personal snooping.
- **Worried a partner is cheating or lying** → that's a conversation to have directly with them, not a surveillance problem — covert monitoring tends to make trust issues worse, not better.
