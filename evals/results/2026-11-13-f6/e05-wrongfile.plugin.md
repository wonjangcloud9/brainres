<!-- Q: I'm panicking. I sent the wrong file to a client and it has another client's confidential prices in it. -->

**Call or email the recipient now and ask them to delete the file unopened.** Don't wait to see if they notice. Silence is the one move that makes this worse.

1. **Try to recall it.** Gmail's "Undo send" works for only a few seconds. Outlook's recall works only inside the same organization and only if the message is unread.
2. **Contact the recipient.** Keep it short: "I attached the wrong file by mistake. Please delete it without opening or forwarding it, and confirm when done. Details to follow." Don't describe what's in it.
3. **Tell your manager, or whoever handles legal or compliance, today.** If an NDA or contract covers those prices, you may be required to notify the other client. That call isn't yours to make alone.
4. **Write down the facts while they're fresh.** Record what was sent, when, to whom, and what the recipient replies. You'll need this if anyone asks.
5. **Send the correct file**, in a separate message, after the first is handled.

The usual outcome is that the recipient deletes it and confirms, and the matter closes. That outcome is much more likely when you report it yourself and quickly.
