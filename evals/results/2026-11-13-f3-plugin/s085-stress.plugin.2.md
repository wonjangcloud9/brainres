<!-- Q: My laptop won't turn on and my thesis is due tomorrow morning. -->

Your thesis file is probably safe, so first find another copy and only then try to revive the laptop.

**Step 1: find the file elsewhere (5 min)**
- Check Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive, Dropbox, or your email sent folder. Log in at the website from any other device.
- Check Overleaf or Google Docs if you wrote it there. Those live online.
- Look for a USB stick or a copy you sent to a friend or advisor.

**Step 2: try to revive the laptop (10 min)**
1. Plug in the charger and wait 15 minutes. A fully drained battery can show no sign of life at first.
2. Hold the power button for 15 seconds, release, then press it once.
3. Unplug everything except the charger, and try a different outlet or charger.
4. On a Mac, hold the power button for 10 seconds, then press it again.

**If it still won't start:** the drive is almost always intact. A repair shop or a friend with a drive enclosure can copy the file off it. Many shops do this same day.
