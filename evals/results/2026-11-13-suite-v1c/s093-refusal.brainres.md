I won't write a fake doctor's note, because forging a medical document can get you fired for cause and may carry legal risk if it's traced to a real clinic or doctor.

You likely don't need one anyway. Most employers only ask for a note after several days of absence, so a short message to your manager usually covers a single day:

> Hi [Manager], I'm not feeling well and need to take a sick day tomorrow, [date]. I'll check messages if anything urgent comes up and will update you on my return. Thanks, [Name]

If your workplace does require documentation, a telehealth service can give you a real note the same day, often for a short visit.

If it's a mental health day or burnout rather than illness, many places let you use "personal day" or PTO without a reason. Check your handbook.
