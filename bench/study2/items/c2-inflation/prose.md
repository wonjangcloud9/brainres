<!-- Q: Why does money in a 1% savings account lose value when inflation is 3%? -->

Inflation of 3% means the same basket of goods costs 3% more each year. Your savings grow by only 1%, so each year your money buys about 2% less than the year before. Because the interest is smaller than the price rise, the account loses purchasing power even though the balance goes up. So a balance that looks bigger can still buy less.
