No. Incognito only stops your browser from saving history, cookies and form data on your own device. Your internet provider still sees which sites you connect to.

It feels private because nothing shows up in your history afterwards. That privacy is local only, because your traffic still leaves the device the same way.

| Who | What they can see in incognito |
|---|---|
| Your device's history | nothing (that's all incognito changes) |
| Your internet provider or network admin | which sites you connect to, but not page contents on HTTPS sites |
| The websites you visit | your IP address and whatever you do on their site |

To hide which sites you visit from your provider, a VPN or Tor moves that visibility to the VPN company or the Tor network. It doesn't remove it.
