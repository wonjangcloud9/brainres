Great question! Incognito mode, also known as private browsing, is a feature available in most modern web browsers such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. It is designed to provide users with a certain level of privacy while browsing the internet.

When you use incognito mode, your browser doesn't save your browsing history, cookies, site data, or information entered in forms. However, it's important to understand that incognito mode does not make you completely anonymous online. There are various parties that may still be able to see your activity, depending on the circumstances.

It's worth noting that there are many misconceptions about incognito mode. If privacy is important to you, you might want to consider additional tools. Feel free to ask if you have any other questions!
