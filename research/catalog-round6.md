| PN-01 | Politeness effect in tutoring | Lab studies found polite tutor feedback ("let's…", "you might…") helped learners, mainly those with low prior knowledge. In a classroom study (n = 132 high-school students), polite feedback gave low-prior-knowledge students no reliable extra benefit, and the effect was weaker than in the lab | Wang, Johnson, Mayer et al. 2008, IJHCS 66; McLaren, DeLeeuw & Mayer 2011, Computers & Education 56 (abstracts checked; lab effect sizes from a secondary wiki, not verified) | C | Partial | When correcting a learner, phrase it collaboratively ("let's check line 5") while still naming the bug plainly.
