# Catalog round 9: what users actually complain about (2026)

Compiled 2026-11-13 in a research round. The backlog was empty, and the measurements and community reports gave nothing new. Prefix `UC-` (user complaints). These are reports of what bothers people, not experiments. Evidence is graded W, and each is checked against brainres's rules and against our saved suite answers.

| ID | Concept | Mechanism | Source | Ev | Applies | Rule | Situation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UC-01 | Undefined or invented terms | Readers complain less about hard words than about terms used without definition: "clique" for group, "IV" for a variable named initialVerification, overused "blast radius" and "load-bearing" | r/ClaudeAI thread, 2026-07-06 (700+ upvotes, 376 comments; via mcp.directory summary) | W | Yes | Define any non-standard term or acronym in one line at first use, whatever the reader's expertise; never coin a label where a plain word works. In our suite the rough heuristic flagged 33/79 brainres vs 37/78 baseline, but a hand check of 20 brainres answers (U1) found only 2 terms the asker likely didn't know (BOM, GIL), and coined phrases like "blast radius" appeared in 0/79 brainres vs 2/78 baseline. Single-turn Q&A shows little of the problem; the complaint was about long plans and specs (U1b) | core |
| UC-02 | Repeated closing offers | "Repeated closing offers" and canned reassurance are named among frontier-model verbal tics. Users also report frustration with option menus at the end | Verbal-tics review, arXiv 2604.19139 (2026; a literature review with no new human study); claudeissues verbosity reports | W | Partial | End with one concrete next step, or the one question needed to proceed; not a menu of extra offers. In our suite, about 40% of answers in both conditions end with an offer (31/79 brainres vs 33/78 baseline; U2 classifies them by hand) | core |
| UC-03 | "Familiar contrasts" ("not X, but Y") | Named as a verbal tic in the same review, and as the top "AI writing" tell in anti-slop lists | arXiv 2604.19139; tropes.fyi | W | Partial | Refute only what the user said or implied; this is already the C16 / `preemptive_refutation` rule | core |
| UC-04 | Multi-section dumps | Answers with 3+ `##` headers draw the most verbosity complaints | claudeissues.com user report (anecdotal) | W | Partial | Already covered: headings only for long answers (`heading_short` check); ≤4 items per group | core |
